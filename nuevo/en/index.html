15 minto move the whole operation to the DR site

The power failed in an operation that can't stop

Sudden power outages hit the primary site of a 24x7 industrial operation.

My call: no blind switchover. We spent a week validating that the DR site could carry the whole operation and, with the client's authorization, made it primary.

Critical infrastructure · 24x7

See result and lesson

Situation

Sudden power outages hit the primary site of a 24x7 industrial operation.

Decision

Before switching over, we spent a week validating that the DR site could carry the entire operation and built the work plan, with continuous reporting to leadership and the client. With the client's authorization we made the DR site primary; I coordinated on-site execution with our infrastructure specialist.

Result

The switchover took 15 minutes. The DR site took over as primary, with a planned return to the original site.

A DR plan without testing is just theory. When the contingency hit, the plan was already validated and documented.

Pharma · Digital transformation

Situation

After a week of daily sessions we measured the gap: what we had delivered covered 60% of expectations, and the budget could not cover the rest.

Decision

Release the MVP already built and develop the missing 40% with the extended-support team, reinforced through an addendum: the client covered 60% of the added cost and the company 40%, keeping the project profitable.

Result

The C-level accepted the final product and it went live.

Product Owner sign-off does not replace alignment with the executive sponsor.

The weather stalled a delivery that depended on satellite imagery

Days before delivery, the weather prevented usable satellite imagery for the GeoMedia processing.

My call: lay out three options with their impact and recommend aerial capture with camera-equipped aircraft. It was approved and we delivered in full.

Geospatial project

See result and lesson

Situation

Without usable satellite imagery, the GeoMedia processing could not be completed.

Decision

The options: archive imagery (ruled out as too old), deliver without that step, or capture new imagery with camera-equipped aircraft. I recommended aerial capture.

Result

Complete project on the committed date.

Risks outside our control get anticipated: having options ready in time is what keeps the date.
